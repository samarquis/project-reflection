# Quality rubric

Score every ticket event, work event, deep review, and self-review from 0-10. Give each dimension 0, 1, or 2. Never average, round, or award unproved credit.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Material claims unsupported or contradicted | Partial proof; consequential gaps remain | Material claims trace to sufficient current evidence |
| Completeness | Required outcome or major source omitted | Required sections present with explicit gaps | Required outcome and all available material sources covered |
| Calibration | Fact, inference, unknown, or causality blurred | Mostly calibrated; minor ambiguity | Facts, inference, unknowns, confidence, and causal limits explicit |
| Actionability | No usable next behavior or proof | Action exists but trigger, owner, or proof is weak | Smallest next action names trigger, owner, and observable proof |
| Learning integration | Prior lessons ignored; promotion unsupported | Lessons considered but application/effect incomplete | Relevant lessons applied/rejected, gate enforced, effectiveness updated |

Required footer:

```markdown
## Quality assessment
- Evidence: <0-2> - <reason>
- Completeness: <0-2> - <reason>
- Calibration: <0-2> - <reason>
- Actionability: <0-2> - <reason>
- Learning integration: <0-2> - <reason>
- Quality score: <sum>/10
- Status: perfect | imperfect
- Deficiencies: <every lost point, or none>
- Path to 10: <missing evidence/actions, or already satisfied>
```

`10/10` and `perfect` require five scores of 2, no known material gap, no blocked required check, and no unresolved contradiction. User request, confidence, effort, or polished prose cannot substitute for proof. Unavailable required evidence forces a score below 10 and an open verification loop.
